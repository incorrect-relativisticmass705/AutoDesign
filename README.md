## 🧠 How AutoDesign Works

AutoDesign uses advanced artificial intelligence to understand what you're trying to communicate. It then applies proven design principles to make your content look clear, professional, and visually appealing.

**Here's what happens behind the scenes:**

1. **It Reads Your Content** - You provide your text, data, or ideas
2. **It Understands the Context** - The program figures out whether it's a research paper, a presentation, or a report
3. **It Applies Design Rules** - Professional typography, color schemes, layout structures, and visual hierarchy
4. **It Learns** - The more you use AutoDesign, the better it gets at understanding your preferences

This isn't just a template filler. AutoDesign makes intelligent design decisions based on your specific content and purpose.
